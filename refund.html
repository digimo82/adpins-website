Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2025  |  Adpins Ltd, Registered in England & Wales

We want every client to be satisfied with our services. If you have a concern about a payment or service delivery, please contact us at info@adpins.ltd before raising a dispute — we will do our best to resolve the issue promptly.

1. DIGITAL SUBSCRIPTIONS & SaaS PLANS

For monthly subscription-based services (AI agents, automation plans, SaaS access, mini app subscriptions):

  • Cancellations made before the next billing cycle will stop future charges.
  • Partial-month refunds are not issued unless the service was not delivered as described.
  • If you experience a technical issue preventing access, contact us within 7 days for a resolution or credit.

2. ONE-TIME DEVELOPMENT SERVICES

For custom software, bot development, and one-time project work:

  • A refund may be issued if work has not yet commenced.
  • Once development work has begun, payments for completed milestones are non-refundable.
  • If the final deliverable does not match the agreed specification, we will revise it at no additional cost.

3. CONSULTING SESSIONS

For paid consulting and strategy sessions:

  • Cancellations made at least 24 hours before the session are fully refundable.
  • Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice are not refundable.
  • If we cancel or reschedule, a full refund will be issued.

4. HOW TO REQUEST A REFUND

To request a refund, please email us at info@adpins.ltd with:

  • Your name and email address used for payment
  • The service or plan you purchased
  • The reason for your refund request

We aim to respond to all refund requests within 2 business days. Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days depending on your payment provider.

5. DISPUTES

We strongly encourage clients to contact us directly before initiating a chargeback or dispute with their bank or card provider. Most issues can be resolved quickly and amicably. Unresolved disputes raised without prior contact may affect future service eligibility.
